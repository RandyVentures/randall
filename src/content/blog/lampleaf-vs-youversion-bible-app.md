I built [Lampleaf](/lampleaf/) because I wanted a Bible app that felt like opening a Bible: one page of Scripture, and beside it something to slow down with. A painting, a voice reading the passage, the original words, or my own notes. YouVersion is the app almost everyone already has, so this is how the two compare.

**Full disclosure:** I make Lampleaf. YouVersion is a great app, and I use it too.

## The short answer

- **Choose YouVersion** if you want the largest choice of translations and languages, reading plans, and features for sharing with friends and church.
- **Choose Lampleaf** if you want a quiet, illustrated reading experience with narration, word study and a journal, offline and with no account.
- **Use both** if you follow plans in YouVersion but want a slower place to read and reflect.

## Side by side

| | Lampleaf | YouVersion |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free; optional one-time Plus purchase | Free |
| **Translations** | BSB, KJV, WEB, side by side | A very large library across many languages |
| **Illustrations** | Classic paintings (Tissot, Doré and others) beside 17 key chapters | Not a focus |
| **Narration** | 57 chapters per translation, verse highlighted as it's read | Audio for selected versions |
| **Original languages** | Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek word by word | Not a focus |
| **Journal** | Beside every verse, with a SOAP template; export to text or PDF | Notes and highlights |
| **Reading plans** | One gentle 7-day plan, no streaks | Thousands of plans |
| **Account** | None; everything stays on your phone | Account for syncing and community |
| **iPhone Duo** | Opens like a book: cover outside, two-page spread inside | Standard app layout |

## What Lampleaf does differently

- **You read beside art.** Classic public-domain paintings sit next to chapters like Genesis 1–3, Psalm 23 and the prodigal son.
- **Narration follows along.** Each verse lights up as it's read, so it works for listening, reading or both.
- **Word study is built in.** Tap into the original Hebrew, Aramaic and Greek with transliteration and meaning.
- **It's private.** No account, no ads, and notes and highlights stay on your device.
- **No guilt.** The reading plan waits for you if you miss a day, and there are no streaks.

## When YouVersion is the better choice

If you want a particular translation that Lampleaf doesn't have, read in a language other than English, follow a long reading plan, or share verses and plans with a group, YouVersion does far more. It's free and well made.
