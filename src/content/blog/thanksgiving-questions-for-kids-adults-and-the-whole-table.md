Thanksgiving dinner has a predictable shape. Ten minutes of "pass the rolls," a few safe questions about work and school, and then either football or the topic everyone promised not to bring up.

A few good questions change that. Not icebreakers for strangers, just the kind that get your own family telling stories you haven't heard. Here are 60, sorted so you can start light and go deeper as the night goes on. Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 this year, so screenshot what you like.

## How to ask without it feeling forced

- **Go first,** and give a real answer. People match the depth of the first answer.
- **Start light.** Funny and this-or-that questions during the meal, gratitude questions over dessert.
- **Let anyone pass.** No one should feel cornered at the table.
- **Ask one follow-up** before moving on: "Wait, what happened next?" is where the stories are.
- **Make it a game.** Passing cards or a phone around feels less like an interview.

## Thanksgiving questions for kids

1. If you could add one new food to Thanksgiving, what would it be?
2. What are you thankful for that's smaller than your hand?
3. If our family had a parade float, what would it look like?
4. What was the best day of your whole year?
5. Which grown-up here would win a pie-eating contest?
6. If the turkey could talk, what would it say?
7. What's something you learned this year that you're proud of?
8. Who's a friend you're thankful for, and why?
9. If you could make one family rule for a day, what would it be?
10. What's the funniest thing that happened at school this year?
11. What do you want to be really good at when you're older?
12. If you could invite anyone in the world to dinner, who would it be?

## Thanksgiving questions for adults

13. What's something you changed your mind about this year?
14. What's a tradition from your childhood you'd bring back?
15. Who's someone you never properly thanked?
16. What's the best advice you got this year?
17. What was harder this year than people knew?
18. What's one small thing that made a big difference for you this year?
19. What did you used to worry about that you don't anymore?
20. What's something you're looking forward to next year?
21. What did your parents get right that you didn't appreciate until later?
22. What's a risk you took that paid off?
23. Who has made your life better this year without knowing it?
24. What would you tell yourself from five years ago?

## Questions for the whole table

25. What's a Thanksgiving memory you still laugh about?
26. What's the worst Thanksgiving dish you've ever eaten?
27. Who in this family tells the best stories?
28. What's one thing that went better this year than you expected?
29. What's a family recipe we should never let go of?
30. If this year were a movie, what would its title be?
31. What's something about this family you'd want your grandkids to know?
32. Where was the best place you went this year?
33. Who taught you something you still use?
34. What's a skill someone at this table has that most of us don't know about?
35. What's the best gift you've ever been given?
36. If we started a new family tradition this year, what should it be?

## Funny Thanksgiving questions

37. What's your most controversial Thanksgiving food opinion?
38. Which side dish could disappear forever and you'd never notice?
39. Who here is most likely to fall asleep before the pie?
40. If you had to be a Thanksgiving food, which one would you be?
41. What's the worst kitchen disaster you've ever caused?
42. Whose Thanksgiving plate would you trust least?
43. If the leftovers had a ranking, what's number one?
44. What's the most ridiculous argument this family has ever had?

## Thanksgiving this-or-that

45. Pumpkin pie or pecan pie?
46. Stuffing or mashed potatoes?
47. White meat or dark meat?
48. Football or a long walk after dinner?
49. Black Friday shopping or staying in pajamas?
50. Cranberry from a can or homemade?
51. Leftover sandwich or leftover plate?
52. Host or guest?

## Gratitude questions for dessert

53. What's something you're thankful for that you didn't have a year ago?
54. Who's someone at this table you're grateful for, and why?
55. What's a hard thing you're grateful for now?
56. What's an ordinary part of your day you'd miss if it were gone?
57. What's a prayer that got answered this year, even if not the way you expected?
58. Who's someone you wish were here tonight? Tell us a story about them.
59. What's one way you want to give back next year?
60. What are you hoping for, for the people at this table?
