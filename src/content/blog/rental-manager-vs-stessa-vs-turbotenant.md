I own and manage my own rentals, and I built [Rental Manager](/rental-manager/) because I wanted a simple, private ledger I could open on my phone. It is not the only good option, and for some landlords it is not the right one. Here is how it compares with the two apps landlords ask me about most, Stessa and TurboTenant.

**Full disclosure:** I make Rental Manager. I've tried to be fair, including where the others do more. Prices come from each company's own pricing page, checked on November 18, 2026.

## The short answer

| If you mainly want to… | Look at |
|---|---|
| List a vacancy, screen tenants and collect rent online | TurboTenant or Stessa |
| Have bookkeeping fill itself in from your bank account | Stessa |
| Keep a private, offline record of rent, expenses and returns, with a Schedule E packet and no subscription | Rental Manager |

## When Stessa is the better choice

Stessa's free plan is generous: unlimited properties, bank feeds, expense and mileage tracking, and online rent collection. If you want transactions to show up on their own and you're comfortable connecting your bank accounts, it saves a lot of typing. The Schedule E report comes with the paid Manage plan. See [Stessa's pricing](https://www.stessa.com/pricing/).

## When TurboTenant is the better choice

TurboTenant is built around the tenant side: listing a vacancy, taking applications, screening and collecting rent. Its free plan covers all of that, and renters pay the screening fee by default. If your biggest headache is finding and managing tenants, start here. See [TurboTenant's pricing](https://www.turbotenant.com/pricing/).

## When Rental Manager is the better choice

Rental Manager is for owners who want the numbers in one place without another subscription or another account holding their records:

- Rent charges, full and partial payments, balances, overdue rent, receipts and tenant statements.
- Expenses, mortgage payments, mileage, deposits, repairs, vendors and receipts, each on the right property.
- Cap rate, cash-on-cash return, equity, operating expense ratio, the 1% rule and the cost of vacancy, per property and across the portfolio.
- A tax-year packet that puts each expense on its Schedule E line, with a PDF for your accountant and a CSV for your records.

It works offline with no account and no ads. It doesn't collect rent, screen tenants or pull in bank transactions, so if you need those, pair it with one of the platforms above or choose one of them instead.

## How I'd decide

1. **Do you need online rent collection or screening?** If yes, sign up for TurboTenant or Stessa's free plan first.
2. **Do you want bookkeeping to happen automatically?** If yes, Stessa.
3. **Do you want a private record of rent, expenses and returns, with a Schedule E packet and no monthly fee?** That is Rental Manager.
